# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Things shrink smaller: valuables and shop items go to 40% of their size instead of half. Configs still on the old 0.5 are updated automatically.
- Big valuables now fit. Anything that would still be longer than 0.8 m in the cart shrinks further until it fits (new setting: Largest size in cart).
- Cosmetic boxes shrink in the cart too (new setting: Shrink cosmetic boxes).

## 1.0.1

- Ducks shrink to half size like gnomes. The earlier 0.3 was too small and left them partly under the floor. Configs still on the old default are updated automatically.
- Added the source link.

## 1.0.0

- First release.
